## Product Boundary
ARC is a lead-response and follow-up SaaS, not a replacement for an entire field-service CRM. Its core capabilities are Missed Call Text Back, Speed to Lead, and Lead Qualification, supported by Lead Sources and a connected Outreach & Follow-Up campaign experience. Treat these as parts of one customer journey, not five disconnected apps or a gallery of AI agents. Distinguish acquiring a new prospect from receiving an inbound inquiry and re-engaging an existing contact. Check contact validity, source, basic fit, and messaging eligibility before communication; learn additional qualifying information through permitted conversations. Do not require a completed qualification interview before the first eligible speed-to-lead response. Do not label imported contacts as leads ARC generated. Borrow the clarity and guided setup of mature tools, not their entire feature lists. Invoicing, dispatch, accounting, job management, a drag-and-drop workflow engine, and building an external lead database are out of scope. Existing necessary capabilities must not be silently removed.

Design for 360px mobile through large desktop, keyboard use, visible focus, and touch. Keep helpful guidance next to consequential business decisions, not explanatory filler around ordinary buttons.

## Portal Structure
Use four primary destinations: Overview, Leads, Campaigns, and Inbox. Put Connections, Business Settings, existing Billing, and Help in secondary navigation; keep unfinished Setup accessible until completed. Lead Sources lives under Leads and is linked contextually from campaign setup. Qualification is configured within the campaign experience rather than becoming another top-level application. Map these destinations onto existing routes where reasonable and preserve valid deep links with compatibility routes when needed. The shell should show the business name, current page, a contextual action, and relevant setup or connection status without a crowded header. Existing Billing means the business's ARC subscription, not invoices for its own customers. Show existing billing data if available; do not invent prices or rebuild payments. Technical operations belong in OPS. The owner should be able to answer: What needs my attention? What is ARC doing? What should I do next?

Save progress through existing authenticated persistence when available. If persistence is missing, label the experience as a preview; do not claim server saving or store real contact data in browser storage. Allow exploration while setup is incomplete and return users to the precise unfinished step. Distinguish account created, configuration saved, connection pending, ready to test, and live. Signup can be immediate; phone provisioning, messaging approval, and real lead activity are separate. Do not promise immediate leads or guaranteed results.

## Campaign Builder
Build a shared guided builder that adapts to the selected campaign, rather than separate incompatible forms. The conceptual steps are Goal and Source, Audience and Rules, Message and Qualification, Timing and Handoff, and Review and Test. Hide irrelevant steps and put optional complexity under Advanced. Every campaign overview should show purpose, source, channel, status, last activity, and the next needed action. Offer create, edit, duplicate-as-draft, preview, and supported pause/resume actions with truthful outcomes; avoid dead buttons. Display an editable plain-language summary such as: 'When an eligible website inquiry arrives, send this response, ask about the requested service, and alert the owner when the lead meets these rules.' Preview messages with sample variables, show missing-variable errors, and disclose estimated usage only when a supported calculation exists. Keep configuration state separate from runtime state: a saved draft is not active, and a paused campaign is not a deleted one. Editing a live configuration should produce reviewable changes rather than silently alter sending behavior. Live activation requires confirmed backend readiness, explicit review, and server authorization; this frontend task does not authorize activating anything.

## Lead Sources
Provide an understandable 'Add lead source' or 'Set up lead sources' flow with three methods: connect incoming inquiries from a supported form/integration; import an existing contact list for an eligible use case; connect an approved prospect provider when one is actually supported. Clearly explain that inbound connections capture demand, imports bring existing contacts into ARC, and providers supply prospects of uncertain intent. Do not imply that all three generate ready-to-buy customers. Show honest available, setup-required, or unavailable states; unsupported methods may explain requirements but cannot report a successful connection. For imports, implement frontend file parsing with the project's established parser, column mapping, row validation, a deduplication preview, source attribution, and eligibility review. Previewing must not enroll anyone or send anything; unsupported production import remains blocked. Retain source and permission provenance. Keep sourced prospects distinguishable from opted-in inquiries. Provider discovery should be a bounded extension point, not an invented integration or scraping project. Recommend a source only when the business context supports it; do not assume residential homeowners are an accessible prospect database.

## Missed Call Recovery
Create a guided Missed Call Text Back campaign with phone connection status, applicable business hours, message template, response timing, repeat-caller handling, owner alerts, and a conversation preview. Explain the supported number-routing arrangement in owner language; do not claim that any existing number can be connected instantly. Readiness must include a tested way to distinguish an unanswered business call from an answered one, including forwarding and voicemail behavior. Include clear scenarios for answered calls, eligible missed calls, repeated calls, unknown callers, unavailable SMS delivery, and failed connections. Model duplicate suppression and a configurable contact cooldown so repeated calls do not trigger repeated texts. Keep the first message relevant to the inquiry; do not automatically enroll the caller in promotional outreach. An inbound call is not proof of permission for every kind of SMS. Show missing eligibility as a blocker with an appropriate next action. Provide a simulation that demonstrates what would happen without placing calls or sending texts.

## Speed And Qualification
For Speed to Lead, guide owners through selecting an incoming source, confirming usable contact fields, choosing an allowed response channel, setting a message, handling after-hours inquiries, and choosing an owner handoff. Design for a fast first eligible response, but do not invent a guaranteed latency. Show duplicate, incomplete, blocked, and failed-delivery scenarios. For qualification, suggest a small number of relevant questions about requested service, location, urgency, and preferred contact timing; avoid a long interrogation. Reuse details already supplied, allow questions to be edited or skipped where appropriate, and separate required fit rules from optional preferences. Outcomes should include Qualified, Needs Information, Outside Service Area, Unsupported Service, and Needs Owner Review, with a readable explanation tied to actual answers. Missing information is not automatic rejection. Never infer sensitive traits or fabricate customer facts, prices, availability, promises, or bookings. Route uncertain or urgent cases to an owner; do not make the assistant an emergency-triage authority. The demo must show one question at a time, qualification updating from replies, and the owner receiving a useful summary.

## Outreach And Follow-Up
Build one connected outreach setup experience for eligible new inquiries, incomplete conversations, and appropriate existing-contact reactivation; keep cold prospecting a distinct source/use case, not the default. Let owners choose the objective, audience, available channel, approved business facts and offers, tone, call to action, follow-up spacing, contact limits, and approval preference. Start with a short editable sequence and restrained defaults, not a large filter builder. Show the personalization preview and the source of each inserted fact. Missing facts should produce a safe fallback or require review, never made-up personalization. AI-generated content is a draft unless an existing approved automation contract explicitly supports autonomous sending. Customer messages and imported text are untrusted inputs, not instructions capable of changing campaign rules. The simulation should show a prospect moving through fit checks, an eligible message, a reply, qualification, and owner handoff. Model opt-out suppression, per-contact limits across campaigns, quiet-hour policy, delivery failure, reply handling, booking, owner takeover, and pause. Replies stop scheduled no-response follow-ups; an allowed conversational assistant may continue only under the configured policy. Do not build or enable actual bulk sending in this phase.

Provide thread history, clear automation-versus-owner authorship, delivery states, a relevant lead summary, and a usable reply composer only where supported. Add take-over and resume controls with clear consequences. A production control must wait for acknowledged backend success before claiming automation is paused or a message sent. ## Overview And Evidence
Make Overview useful before and after activation. A new account should see its setup progress and the next relevant action, not fabricated performance. An active account should see a compact attention queue, campaign status, recent meaningful activity, and a small set of explainable metrics such as eligible new inquiries, median first-response time, engaged missed callers, and qualified leads. Include booking data only when there is an authoritative source or an explicitly labeled owner report. Define each metric, time window, denominator, exclusions, and source in accessible detail. Separate accepted, sent, delivered, replied, qualified, and booked events. A missed caller who replies is an engaged inquiry, not automatically a recovered job. Unknown data is not zero; no observations means 'Not enough data,' not a perfect response time. Do not calculate revenue as lead count times an assumed job value. No unverified 'revenue generated' or 'hours saved' counters. Useful drilldowns should lead to the supporting records. Recommendations should reflect real account state and offer one relevant action, such as testing an unverified source, rather than generic AI advice.
